The full-height navy `brand` column on the left edge of every page: home link at the top, section name down the middle.

- **Provide**: `subject` (the article's section, or `Meaning` on wiki pages) and `homeHref` / `sectionHref`.
- **Container**: the spine is fixed to the left edge and fills the window from top to bottom. The page leaves room for it, so the masthead and footer stop at its left edge.
- Keep the spine on the left, away from the site card at the top right, so the brand never repeats side by side.
- In a narrow or short window it goes away, and the inline wordmark moves into the top bar as a pill.
