The publication's name set in Anton capitals, with "on" stamped across it in a tilted seal; the only logo the brand has.

- **Provide**: `layout` (`inline` for nav bars and footers, `stacked` inside the sticker and spine, `vertical` down a spine), an optional `subject` to name a section ("Agents" renders *Machines on Agents*), and a `size`.
- **"On" is the biggest thing in the name.** It is set at `wordmark-on-scale` (1.35 times MACHINES) inside a round seal `wordmark-seal-size` across, tilted `wordmark-seal-tilt` (-8deg). The proportion is the same in every layout and at every size, from the 17px nav pill to the 92px sticker.
- **The seal sits behind the words.** It may overlap MACHINES and the subject, but it never covers a letter: the words are drawn on top of it. Inline, it sits between the words; stacked, it straddles the two lines, indented; vertical, it sits in the column between the words.
- Where a column limits the seal (the spine), shrink the whole wordmark rather than "on" alone.
- Use `subject` only for section and wiki naming; the site itself is always the whole name in the page's language.
- **The name is localized** (Ryan, 2026-11-15: "We will localize the branding, fully"). A language edition draws its own lockup from its interface strings (`brand.lead`, `brand.on`, `site.name`): Spanish *Máquinas SOBRE Máquinas*, Chinese *机器 论 机器* (`lead` and `on` props). A middle word longer than two letters (`sobre`) sits in a **pill** instead of the round seal: same height, tilt and colour, stretched along the line of text (`mom-wordmark-on-wide`, tokens `wordmark-on-wide-scale` and `wordmark-seal-wide-height`). A single Chinese character (`论`) keeps the round seal. Chinese is set in heavy CJK display strokes, normal style, no letter-spacing, no capitals. Every layout (inline, stacked, vertical, the sticker, the spine, the footer, the loop bands) is drawn from the same words; never an image. The favicon and app icon stay the language-neutral *ON* mark. The share picture is drawn per language by `tools/build_share_images.py`.
- Do not recolor the seal: it is always `human` with `on-human` text, except inside the spine's section name, where it is `paper` with `ink`.
- Never render the wordmark as an image, outline it, or put a shadow behind it.
