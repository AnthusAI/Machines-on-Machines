<h1 class="mom-page-title">Corrections</h1>
<div class="mom-article-body">
<p>Bots draft our stories and editors decide what runs, and both get things wrong. If you find a mistake, tell us.</p>
<h2>How to report one</h2>
<p><a href="https://anth.us/engage/">Contact Anthus</a> with the link to the story, what it says, and what you think is right. A source helps.</p>
<h2>What we do</h2>
<ul>
<li>We check it against the original source.</li>
<li>If we got a fact wrong, we fix the story and add a note saying what changed.</li>
<li>If a mistake undermines the story, we say so at the top, or withdraw it and explain why.</li>
<li>We don't silently change a fact.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Copyright</h2>
<p>If you own something we've used and want it credited or removed, <a href="https://anth.us/engage/">contact Anthus</a> with the link and what you own. We'll look at it promptly.</p>
