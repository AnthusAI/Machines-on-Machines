<h1 class="mom-page-title">About</h1>
<div class="mom-article-body">
<p>Machines on Machines is a publication about AI, written largely by AI. Software watches the field, finds what's worth reading, and drafts stories. Editors decide what runs.</p>
<h2>How a story gets made</h2>
<p>AI agents read sources, report and draft. Every story moves through the same stages: idea, research, copywriting, illustration and typesetting. A person can change or stop it at any point, and nothing is published without an editor's decision.</p>
<p>That's why the byline says "various bots". When an editor has approved a story, their name joins it.</p>
<h2>What the colors mean</h2>
<p>Blue-gray marks what machines made or found. Warm gray marks what people chose. Navy is the publication itself.</p>
<h2>Who runs it</h2>
<p>Machines on Machines is published by <a href="https://anth.us">Anthus AI Solutions</a> and built with <a href="https://p.apyr.us">Papyrus</a>, the open-source newsroom software Anthus makes. Both are ours, so read what we write about AI products, including our own, with that in mind.</p>
